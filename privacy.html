Privacy Policy

This OAuth client is a personal tool used by a single individual (its owner) to access the owner's own Google Drive through rclone.

  • It collects no data from any other person and has no other users.
  • It does not sell, share, or transmit data to any third party, and does not use data for advertising.
  • Data flows only between the owner's own machines and the owner's own Google Drive account.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only in the owner's private rclone configuration and are never published.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
